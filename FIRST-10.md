# First 10 — do these on the live site

1. Sign in with work email.
2. Add one real lead.
3. Open the lead file and change status.
4. Schedule an inspection from that file.
5. Start inspection and upload photos.
6. Open Inspections and confirm the session.
7. Draft a report from that inspection. Do not send to the homeowner.
8. Save labor rates and tax in Settings.
9. Add a task on that lead.
10. Open Brief. Open Passport / Warranty after migration 020.
