# Sales to production job handoff (Day 14)

Migration: `supabase/migrations/064_production_job_handoff.sql`.
Code: `lib/jobs/production.ts`, `app/api/estimates/[id]/job/route.ts`, `app/estimate/[id]/page.tsx`.

## What this is

One production job created from one estimate that has both:

1. an owner-approved estimate, and
2. a recorded customer acceptance (Day 13).

The job is the production handoff record. It keeps an immutable snapshot of the
customer, the accepted amount and the approved scope.

## Database rules

The database enforces every handoff rule:

1. The estimate must be `approved`.
2. The estimate must have a customer (`lead_id`).
3. The acceptance must exist and belong to the same estimate and workspace.
4. The accepted total must be greater than zero.
5. The lead must not be `lost`.
6. The estimate must have at least one line item.
7. The job number is generated by the database (`JOB-` plus a UUID fragment).
8. `lead_id`, `customer_name`, `property_address`, `accepted_total`,
   `accepted_at`, `estimate_formula_version`, `approved_scope` and
   `scope_item_count` are copied by the database. Client-supplied values are
   overwritten.
9. There is one job per estimate (`unique (estimate_id)`).
10. The lead becomes `won` with the next action `Prepare production job`.
11. Source evidence is immutable. `UPDATE` of any source field raises an error.
12. `DELETE` raises an error; cancel the job instead.
13. Status moves follow:
    - `ready` to `scheduled`, `in_progress` or `cancelled`
    - `scheduled` to `in_progress` or `cancelled`
    - `in_progress` to `completed` or `cancelled`
    - `completed` and `cancelled` are terminal.
14. RLS is workspace-scoped. The creator must be the signed-in member.

## What this is not

- No crew assignment, material schedule, permit or weather gate. Those are Day 20–21.
  (Crew and material planning now exists separately: [production-schedule.md](./production-schedule.md). It is operational only and does not change this handoff.)
- No invoice, payment or change order.
- No automatic customer or insurer contact.
- No mock approval. The handoff needs a real approved estimate and a recorded acceptance in the same workspace.
